## How to Talk to Your Agents

Open Claude Code (the terminal application on your desktop) and just type naturally. Here are examples:

### Daily Operations
- **"Brief me"** — Get a morning summary of your emails, calendar, and priorities
- **"What's on my calendar today?"** — See today's schedule
- **"Schedule a callback with John Smith tomorrow at 2pm"** — Create calendar events

### Customer Communications
- **"Draft a reply to this customer asking about pricing"** — Get a professional email draft
- **"Write a follow-up thank you for the Smiths"** — After-service follow-up
- **"Respond to this review"** — Draft a professional review response

### Lead Management
- **"Score this lead: [paste the inquiry]"** — Get a priority score and recommended action
- **"What new leads came in today?"** — Check recent inquiries

### Tips
- Be specific — "Draft a reply about our drain cleaning pricing" works better than "write something"
- Always review before sending — your agents create drafts, not final sends
- Say "brief me" every morning to start your day organized
- If something looks wrong, just say "that's not right" and explain what to fix

---

## Important Rules

1. **Always review before acting** — Your agents create drafts and recommendations. YOU make the final decision on everything.

2. **Nothing sends automatically** — No emails, posts, or messages go out without your approval.

3. **Your data stays on your machine** — All your business info, configs, and memory are stored locally on your computer.

4. **AI isn't perfect** — Occasionally an agent might get something wrong. That's normal. Just correct it and move on.

5. **Don't share sensitive passwords in chat** — Your connections (Gmail, Calendar) are set up securely through tokens. You don't need to type passwords into Claude Code.
