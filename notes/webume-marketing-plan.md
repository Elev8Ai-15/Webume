## Niche Analysis

### Market Landscape
The AI-powered resume builder market was valued between $400M and $1.3B in 2024, growing at 15-20% CAGR, projected to reach $1.8B+ by 2032. The broader resume builder market is expected to reach $11.95B by 2029. Demand for AI resume tools surged over 10,900% YoY as of 2025.

### Defensible Differentiators
1. **Organic Chronological Career Tree** — 8-section interactive experience per employer (category-defining)
2. **Full PWA with app store readiness** — No competitor in app stores
3. **AI Resume Tailor** — Match scoring + keyword analysis + tailored summaries in single flow
4. **Industry-specific templates** — Healthcare, Restaurant, Trades, Beauty, Nonprofit, and more
5. **Price-to-value ratio** — $9.99/mo undercuts all major competitors

## Strategic Roadmap

### Phase 1: Foundation (Months 1-3)
- Submit to Google Play + Microsoft Store
- Launch blog with SEO content
- Set up analytics, email marketing, social presence
- Begin Reddit engagement, TikTok content

### Phase 2: Growth (Months 4-6)
- Submit to Apple App Store
- Launch "State of Resumes 2026" report
- A/B test pricing, implement upgrade prompts
- First university pilot, staffing agency outreach

### Phase 3: Scale (Months 7-12)
- Scale paid acquisition to $7K/mo
- Enterprise landing page with case studies
- HR Tech conferences, expand university partnerships
- Launch cover letter builder, affiliate program

### Phase 4: Market Leadership (Months 13-18)
- Multi-language support (Spanish, French)
- LinkedIn optimization feature
- Strategic partnerships with major job boards
- Target $15K MRR, evaluate fundraising
