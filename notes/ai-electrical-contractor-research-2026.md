## 1. AI-Powered Load Calculations & Panel Sizing

### What Exists Today
- **Kopperfield**: Snap a photo of an electrical panel, AI populates items for load calculations automatically. Includes NEC chatbot, panel schedules, circuit labels. Eco Energy Services reports generating load calcs in **5 minutes** with the tool.
- **Qmerit Panel Insights**: Evaluates panel specs from a single image -- available circuit spaces, tandem breakers, total capacity. Built with Schneider Electric's AI Hub. Qmerit raised **$20.8M**, acquired by Schneider Electric in 2021.
- **Siemens Electrical Designer**: Free AI-powered tool for automated electrical design, short-circuit checks, and cable sizing.
- **EleCalculator**: Free NEC Article 220 load calculator for residential, commercial, and industrial.

### What a Small AI Company Could Build
- A mobile-first app where electricians photograph a panel, AI reads the breakers, calculates remaining capacity, and recommends upgrades for EV chargers, heat pumps, or solar
- Integration with local utility rate schedules to show cost impact
- Automated NEC 220 demand calculation with export to permit-ready PDF
- White-label version for electrical supply houses to offer their contractor customers

### Key Data Points
- EV charger installations alone require load calculations on every job -- this market is exploding
- Panel photo AI processing is still early-stage; Kopperfield and Qmerit are the main players
- Most small contractors still do load calcs by hand or with basic spreadsheets

## 3. NEC Code Compliance Checking via AI

### What Exists Today
- **Kopperfield NEC Chatbot**: Free, trained on 2017, 2020, and 2023 NEC editions. Instant code lookups, calculation help, exam prep.
- **NEC-spert**: GPT-based AI assistant for NEC compliance, safety analysis, code interpretation
- **ElectriCalc Pro**: Voice-activated NEC code searches, project-specific safety checklists, offline functionality
- **CodeGenie**: Plain-language NEC lookups, returns relevant articles with explanations
- **ComplianceAI**: Monitors jurisdiction-specific code changes, alerts on updates affecting projects
- **Captain Code App (Leviton)**: NEC code changes with expert analysis from IAEI

### What a Small AI Company Could Build
- A jurisdiction-aware code compliance checker: input your city/county, and the AI knows which NEC edition is adopted locally plus any local amendments
- Pre-inspection checklist generator: describe the job scope, AI generates a complete inspection checklist based on applicable codes
- Photo-based compliance review: electrician photographs their work, AI flags potential code violations before the inspector arrives
- Integration with permit applications: auto-populate code references on permit forms
- Real-time code change alerts: when a jurisdiction adopts a new NEC edition, notify affected contractors

### Key Data Points
- NEC code adoption varies by state -- only some states are on 2023 NEC, many still on 2020 or 2017
- Failed inspections cost contractors **$500-$2,000+** per occurrence in rework and scheduling delays
- Code compliance is the #1 concern for apprentice and journeyman electricians
- This is a high-value, low-competition niche -- most existing tools are basic chatbots

### Key Data Points

- ELECTRI International published an AI Implementation Roadmap specifically for electrical contractors

## 5. Automated Permit Tracking & Scheduling

### What Exists Today
- **PermitFlow**: Raised **$54 million** for AI-powered permitting. Auto-generates and files permits in minutes. Research Agent searches permit databases and AHJ portals.
- **Fieldproxy**: AI field service management for electrical contractors. Savings of **$3,000-$8,000/month** for mid-sized firms (10-15 technicians). Includes permit tracking and inspection scheduling.
- **EMOR AI**: Tracks permit status, schedules inspections, integrates with ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Jobber.
- **Datagrid AI Agents**: 80% less administrative work, 3-week faster permit approvals. Real-time permit status monitoring across jurisdictions.

### What a Small AI Company Could Build
- A lightweight permit tracker built specifically for small electrical contractors (not enterprise construction)
- Features: permit application status dashboard, inspection scheduling with calendar sync, automated follow-up reminders, document storage per job
- AI enhancement: predict permit approval timelines based on historical data for that jurisdiction
- Auto-fill permit applications using job details already entered
- Inspector preference notes: track which inspectors cover which areas, their common callouts, scheduling preferences
- Integration with Google Calendar and common FSM tools

### Key Data Points
- Permit expeditors spend **60% of their time** on data entry and phone calls
- Manual permit tracking adds **$50,000+** to project costs through labor delays
- **VelocityEHS** reports up to **90% reduction** in administrative burden with electronic permit management
- Most small electrical contractors track permits on paper, whiteboards, or basic spreadsheets

## 6. AI Chatbot for Customer Intake (Photo Upload + Issue Description)

### What Exists Today
- **AgentiveAIQ**: No-code AI chatbot for electrical services. Starts at **$39/month**. Two-agent architecture (customer-facing + background intelligence). Pro plan at $129/month, Agency plan at $449/month.
- **EMOR AI**: AI answering that integrates with ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Jobber
- **Copilot.Live**: AI chatbot specifically for electricians, handles inquiries 24/7, schedules appointments
- **Ada**: Enterprise-grade but expensive ($1,500/month starter)

### What a Small AI Company Could Build
- An AI intake chatbot specifically for electrical contractors with these features:
  - Customer describes issue in plain language ("my outlet is sparking," "lights keep flickering")
  - Photo/video upload: customer photographs the problem area
  - AI triages: emergency vs. routine, residential vs. commercial, estimated complexity
  - Auto-generates work order with preliminary diagnosis for the dispatcher
  - Schedules appointment based on availability and urgency
  - Sends automated confirmation with "what to expect" and preparation tips
  - After-hours emergency routing: AI determines if it's a true emergency (fire risk, exposed wiring) vs. can wait
- White-label for contractor branding
- Integration with existing scheduling tools

### Key Data Points
- Duke Energy's AI chatbot handled **280,000 interactions in 3 months**, reduced manual submissions by **90%**
- Utility companies projected to save **$8 billion** through AI chatbot implementation
- Most electrical contractors miss **30-40% of incoming calls** during business hours (crews are on-site)
- After-hours calls are the highest-converting leads -- AI chatbot captures them 24/7
- Photo upload for issue description is a gap in the market -- no major player has nailed this yet

## 7. Predictive Job Costing Based on Historical Data

### What Exists Today
- **BuildOps**: AI-driven quotes using real-time material pricing, historic job data, labor benchmarks
- **Drawer AI**: Raised **$5M seed funding** (led by Brick & Mortar Ventures). Cuts takeoff time by **70%**. Starr Electric achieved 70% time savings analyzing 2,600+ fixtures.
- **PataBid Quantify**: AI handles **80% of takeoff work in 20% of the time**. Table Extractor reads panel schedules, lighting schedules, equipment lists from PDFs.
- **Beam**: Connects AI estimating to budgets and job costing -- no double data entry.

### What a Small AI Company Could Build
- A job costing intelligence platform for small electrical contractors:
  - Import historical job data (even from QuickBooks, Excel, or handwritten records)
  - AI learns the contractor's actual costs: labor hours by job type, material usage patterns, markup tendencies
  - For new bids: input job description, AI predicts total cost, labor hours, material list, and suggested price
  - Tracks actual vs. estimated on completed jobs to improve accuracy over time
  - Material price alerts: monitors copper, conduit, wire prices and adjusts estimates in real-time
  - Win/loss tracking: correlate bid amounts with win rates to find the pricing sweet spot
- Competitive advantage: most small contractors estimate from gut feel; this gives them data-backed pricing

### Key Data Points
- AI estimations achieve **97% accuracy rate**, significantly reducing cost overruns
- AI-assisted takeoff reduces estimating time by **40%**, enabling **68 bids/year vs. 52 manual bids**
- Electrical contractors typically win only **10-20% of bids** submitted
- Copper wire and EMT conduit prices can swing **15-25%** within weeks
- Most small contractors still estimate using mental math, basic spreadsheets, or outdated software

## 8. Solar Panel ROI Calculators Powered by AI

### What Exists Today
- **Google Project Sunroof**: Maps roof solar potential using satellite imagery
- **EasySolar**: AI automates NEC-compliant electrical diagrams, permit-ready documents with one click
- **ToolGrit**: Free solar array sizing calculator for installers and electricians
- **PVGIS24**: Solar production simulations with professional reports
- **Enphase**: Guides and tools for solar ROI calculation

### What a Small AI Company Could Build
- A contractor-facing solar proposal tool:
  - Input: customer address, monthly electric bill, roof orientation, shading
  - AI pulls: satellite imagery, local solar irradiance data, utility rate schedules, available incentives (federal ITC, state rebates, SREC markets)
  - Output: system size recommendation, estimated production, 25-year savings projection, payback period, financing options
  - Generates a branded PDF proposal the electrician can present on-site
  - Includes panel upgrade requirements if load calc shows need
  - Battery storage add-on analysis (Tesla Powerwall, Enphase, etc.)
  - Tracks proposal-to-close ratio for sales optimization
- Integration with solar distributors for real-time equipment pricing

### Key Data Points
- Federal solar ITC: **30% tax credit** for systems installed through 2032
- Average residential solar installation: **$12,000-$36,000** before incentives
- Typical payback period: **7-10 years** with ITC, **~13 years** without
- Solar panels increase home values by **4.1% (Zillow)** to **10% (SolarInsure)**
- Average solar ROI in favorable markets: **10-20% annual return**
- Many electricians are adding solar to their service offerings -- this tool helps them compete with dedicated solar companies
