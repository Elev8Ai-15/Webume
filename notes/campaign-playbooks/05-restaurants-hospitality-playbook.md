## A. Industry Pain Points (Top 5)

### 1. Phone Overwhelm During Peak Hours
**The problem:** During lunch and dinner rushes, the phone is ringing for reservations, takeout orders, catering inquiries, and basic questions like "What are your hours?" and "Do you have vegan options?" The host or manager is pulled away from serving guests to answer repetitive calls. Many calls go unanswered during the busiest -- and most profitable -- hours.

**How AI solves it:** An AI chatbot and phone system handles reservation requests, answers FAQs (hours, menu, dietary options, parking), takes catering inquiries, and routes urgent calls to staff. The front-of-house team stays focused on the guests who are already there and spending money.

### 2. Reservation and Event Booking Friction
**The problem:** Many restaurants and venues still take reservations by phone or through clunky third-party systems that charge per cover. Event venues lose inquiries because the booking process requires multiple back-and-forth emails. Every point of friction between "I want to book" and "I am booked" is where customers drop off.

**How AI solves it:** AI-powered booking on the restaurant or venue's website allows guests to make reservations, inquire about private events, and get instant answers about availability -- 24/7, no phone call needed, no third-party fees.

### 3. Online Reputation Can Make or Break the Business
**The problem:** One bad Yelp or Google review can cost a restaurant thousands. Meanwhile, the hundreds of happy customers who dine there every week rarely leave reviews unless prompted. Restaurant owners know reviews matter but do not have a consistent system to generate them.

**How AI solves it:** Automated post-visit text messages thanking guests and asking for a review. AI routes happy guests to Google/Yelp and routes unhappy guests to a private feedback form so the manager can address concerns before they go public. Reviews accumulate naturally and the rating trends upward.

### 4. Staffing Shortages and High Turnover
**The problem:** The hospitality industry has the highest turnover rate of any sector -- over 70% annually. Every new hire means weeks of training. Every departure means knowledge loss and service gaps. Finding reliable front-desk staff, hosts, or event coordinators in Tampa Bay is a constant battle.

**How AI solves it:** AI does not replace people, but it absorbs the tasks that make staffing so painful. When the host position is empty, the chatbot still books reservations. When the event coordinator is on vacation, the AI still captures wedding inquiries. The business is no longer held hostage by who is or is not on the schedule.

### 5. Catering and Event Leads Slip Through the Cracks
**The problem:** Catering and private events are the highest-margin opportunities for restaurants and venues. But these inquiries often come via website forms, DMs, or voicemail -- and they require multiple touchpoints to close. Without a follow-up system, these high-value leads go cold. One lost wedding booking or corporate event can be $5,000-$50,000+ in lost revenue.

**How AI solves it:** AI captures every catering and event inquiry instantly, asks qualifying questions (date, guest count, budget, event type), and sends the details to the events team in a structured format. Automated follow-up sequences keep the lead warm until the deal is closed.
