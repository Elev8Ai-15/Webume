## How to Use These Playbooks

1. **Pick a target industry** for the month based on pipeline and opportunity
2. **Record HeyGen videos** using the scripts (batch 5-10 at a time with personalization)
3. **Run LinkedIn DM sequences** on 10-15 prospects per week
4. **Launch cold email sequences** to 20-30 prospects per week
5. **Rotate Facebook/Instagram ads** with 2-week testing cycles
6. **Post content** 3-5x per week mixing across industries for variety
7. **Use the assessment questions** in your AI Readiness Assessment funnel

## Key Messaging Pillars (All Industries)

- **Scarcity:** Only 3 client spots per month -- this is real and drives urgency
- **Local:** Tampa Bay based, local business owner helping local business owners
- **Proof:** Lead with results, not features
- **Personal:** Brad's face, Brad's voice, Brad's story -- never generic
- **Value-first:** Educate before you sell, every single time

## Personalization Variables Used Across All Playbooks

- `[FIRST NAME]` -- prospect's first name
- `[BUSINESS NAME]` -- prospect's business name
- `[CITY/AREA]` -- their specific Tampa Bay area (Clearwater, St. Pete, Tampa, etc.)
- `[INDUSTRY]` -- their specific sub-industry
- `[SPECIFIC PAIN POINT]` -- something you noticed about their business
- `[WEBSITE URL]` -- their current website
- `[COMPETITOR]` -- a local competitor who is doing well with AI/automation
