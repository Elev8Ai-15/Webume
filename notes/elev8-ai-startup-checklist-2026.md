## 3. LEGAL & BUSINESS FORMATION

### Formation (Florida)
- [ ] Florida LLC — Sunbiz.org ($125)
- [ ] EIN — IRS.gov (free)
- [ ] Operating Agreement (self-draft or attorney $200-800)
- [ ] Florida Annual Report ($138.75/yr — due May 1, $400 late fee!)
- [ ] Business bank account (free)
- [ ] Registered Agent (be your own = free, or $100-300/yr)

### Contracts & Legal Docs
- [ ] Master Services Agreement (MSA) — attorney $500-1,500 or template
- [ ] Statement of Work (SOW) template
- [ ] NDA (mutual) — Rocket Lawyer ($40/mo) or template
- [ ] Terms of Service — Termageddon ($12/mo) or attorney
- [ ] Privacy Policy — Termageddon or Termly (free basic)
- [ ] Independent Contractor Agreement
- [ ] IP Assignment Agreement

### AI-Specific Contract Clauses
- Specify target accuracy RANGES, not guarantees
- Include clause for third-party platform changes (OpenAI/Anthropic API changes)
- Distinguish client-specific work vs. your reusable IP/frameworks
- Request 25-40% upfront for new clients
- Good-faith renegotiation clause for scope changes

### Insurance
- [ ] Professional Liability (E&O) — $500-1,500/yr
- [ ] General Liability — $400-800/yr
- [ ] Cyber Liability — $500-1,000/yr
- Recommended: Hiscox, Next Insurance, or TechInsurance

**Estimated: $625-3,500 one-time + $150-350/yr ongoing**

## 6. CLIENT DELIVERY & OPERATIONS

### Onboarding Process (Day 1-7)
1. [ ] Welcome email — contract, SOW, onboarding questionnaire
2. [ ] Kick-off call — confirm scope, KPIs, timeline, comms preferences
3. [ ] Set up shared workspace — PM board, shared folder, Slack channel
4. [ ] Collect access/credentials — APIs, logins, data sources
5. [ ] Define "first win" — quick result in week 1-2

### Project Workflow
| Phase | Duration | Deliverables |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| Discovery & Audit | 1-2 weeks | Assessment, opportunity map, feasibility |
| Design & Architecture | 1-2 weeks | Solution design, tech stack, project plan |
| Build & Pilot | 2-6 weeks | MVP/prototype, iterative dev, demos |
| Test & Refine | 1-2 weeks | QA, user testing, performance tuning |
| Deploy & Train | 1 week | Production deploy, docs, team training |
| Support & Optimize | Ongoing | Monitoring, fixes, optimization |

### Communication Standards
- Weekly status updates (email or Loom video)
- Bi-weekly check-in calls (30 min max)
- Response SLA: 24 hrs non-urgent, 4 hrs urgent

### Support/Maintenance Tiers
| Tier | Monthly | Includes |
|------|---------|---------|
| Basic | $500-1,000 | Bug fixes, monitoring, 4 hrs support |
| Standard | $1,000-3,000 | + optimizations, 10 hrs, monthly strategy call |
| Premium | $3,000-5,000+ | + priority response, 20+ hrs, weekly calls, new features |

## 8. TEAM & SCALING

### Phase 1: Solo ($0-10K/mo revenue)
- You do everything
- Use freelancers for overflow
- Focus: land first 3-5 clients, build case studies

### Phase 2: Solo + Contractors ($10K-25K/mo)
- 1-2 freelance developers for delivery
- Part-time VA ($5-15/hr) for admin
- Platforms: Upwork, Contra, Toptal, Fiverr Pro

### Phase 3: Small Team ($25K-50K+/mo)
- First hire: Delivery lead / senior dev ($80K-140K)
- Second hire: Operations / PM ($50K-70K)
- Keep contractors for specialized skills

### Hire Priority Order
1. AI/ML Developer (contractor first)
2. Project Manager / Ops (part-time VA first)
3. Sales / BD (commission-based first)
4. Content / Marketing (freelancer $25-75/hr)
5. Designer (freelancer $30-75/hr)
