10. **Checkout/payment system** — e-commerce ready with credit/debit card processing
11. **FAQ section** — addresses common objections
12. **5-star rating** — social proof (even if only 3 reviews)
13. **ADA scanner tool** — unique differentiator, shows you practice what you preach
14. **Gemini integration page** — shows AI-native approach

### What's MISSING or Needs Improvement 🔧

#### HIGH PRIORITY (Do These First)

1. **No blog content** — Blog page exists but appears empty
   - WHY IT MATTERS: Blog = SEO fuel. Google rewards fresh, relevant content. You're leaving organic traffic on the table.
   - FIX: Write 2 posts/month minimum. Topics: "How AI saves plumbers $47K/year," "5 AI tools every real estate agent needs," etc.

2. **Portfolio page appears empty** — Page exists at /portfolio but no visible case studies or work examples
   - WHY IT MATTERS: Prospects need proof. "Show, don't tell" is everything in consulting.
   - FIX: Add 3-5 case studies with before/after results, screenshots, and testimonials. Even mock-ups of what you CAN build count.

3. **Only 3 reviews** — Not enough social proof
   - WHY IT MATTERS: 84% of people trust online reviews as much as personal recommendations
   - FIX: Ask every client/contact for a Google review. Target 15+ reviews within 60 days.

4. **No booking/calendar integration visible** — No Calendly, Cal.com, or scheduling tool detected
   - WHY IT MATTERS: "Free AI assessment" with no instant booking = friction. People bounce.
   - FIX: Embed Calendly or Cal.com on the assessment page. Let people book a 15-min call instantly.

5. **No live chat/chatbot on the site** — You sell AI chatbots but don't have one on your own site
   - WHY IT MATTERS: This is your #1 demo opportunity. "Eat your own cooking."
   - FIX: Deploy Victoria (your voice agent) or a chat widget on every page. This alone could be your best sales tool.

6. **Pricing is vague** — Only "$1,299 setup" mentioned, no tier breakdown
   - WHY IT MATTERS: Business owners want to self-qualify before calling. Vague pricing creates hesitation.
   - FIX: Create 3 clear tiers (Starter / Growth / Enterprise) with feature lists and monthly pricing

#### MEDIUM PRIORITY

7. **No video content** — No demo videos, explainer videos, or testimonial videos
   - FIX: This is exactly what your Grok video scripts are for! Embed them on service pages.

8. **No lead magnet beyond assessment** — Need downloadable resources
   - FIX: Create a "Free AI Readiness Checklist" PDF or "10 Ways AI Can Save Your Business $50K/Year" guide

9. **No email capture/newsletter signup** — Missing ongoing nurture opportunity
   - FIX: Add email signup with a valuable freebie. Use for drip campaigns.

10. **Contact page lacks a form** — Just phone and email, no web form detected
    - FIX: Add a simple contact form with name, email, business type, and "What do you need help with?"

11. **No "Results" or "ROI" page** — You have incredible stats from your research
    - FIX: Create a page showing real industry stats: "Restaurants save $47K/year," "Plumbers capture 112 after-hours leads in 3 months"

12. **Service area could be broader** — Only Tampa Bay listed
    - FIX: AI consulting can be done remotely. Add "Serving Tampa Bay locally + businesses nationwide remotely"

13. **No LinkedIn or other social links** — Only Facebook found
    - FIX: Add LinkedIn, TikTok, Instagram, YouTube icons in the footer

#### NICE TO HAVE

14. **No client logos section** — Even if early stage, show tools/partners you work with (OpenAI, Anthropic, Twilio, etc.)
15. **No "How It Works" section** — Simple 3-step process graphic builds confidence
16. **No urgency/scarcity elements** — "Limited spots available" or "Book this month, save 20%"
17. **No comparison table** — "Us vs. hiring a developer" or "Us vs. DIY AI tools"
18. **No trust badges** — SSL, payment security, satisfaction guarantee icons
19. **No accessibility statement page** — You offer ADA compliance but should have your own statement
20. **Blog categories needed** — When you add blog content, organize by industry (real estate, plumbing, restaurants, etc.)
