## RECITALS

WHEREAS, Powell possesses expertise in artificial intelligence application design, concept development, and product strategy;

WHEREAS, Spaulding possesses expertise in software development, coding, marketing, and advertising;

WHEREAS, both Partners desire to enter into a business partnership for the purpose of developing, marketing, and commercializing software applications and AI-based products;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements herein, the Partners agree as follows:

---

## ARTICLE 1 — PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.1 The purpose of this Partnership is to jointly develop, market, advertise, and commercialize software applications and AI-based products and services ("Projects").

1.2 This Agreement covers all current Projects listed in Exhibit A and all future Projects developed jointly or individually under the Partnership.

---

## ARTICLE 2 — ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

2.1 **Brad Powell / Elev8 AI Solutions & Services LLC** shall serve as the Application Specialist and shall be responsible for:
- App concept development and product design
- Product strategy and vision
- Client-facing representation of Elev8 AI brand
- Manages all final aesthetics, creative, and product decisions

2.2 **Francis Spaulding / Spaulding Financial Services LLC** shall serve as the Developer and shall be responsible for:
- All software development and coding
- Marketing strategy and execution
- Advertising campaigns
- Technical infrastructure and deployment

2.3 Both Partners agree to devote reasonable time, effort, and resources necessary to fulfill their respective roles.

---

## ARTICLE 3 — FINANCIAL TERMS

3.1 **Revenue Split.** All gross revenue and net profits generated from the Projects shall be divided equally between the Partners on a **50/50 basis**.

3.2 **Gross Revenue** means all income received before deduction of any expenses.

3.3 **Net Profit** means gross revenue minus all agreed-upon, documented business expenses.

3.4 **Payment Schedule.** Revenue distributions shall occur no less than once per calendar month, or as otherwise mutually agreed in writing.

3.5 **Expenses.** Any business expense exceeding $[AMOUNT — to be agreed] must be pre-approved in writing by both Partners before being incurred.

3.6 **Banking.** A joint business bank account shall be established within 30 days of the execution of this Agreement. Both Partners shall have equal access and signing authority.

3.7 **Accounting.** The Partnership shall retain a mutually agreed-upon third-party accounting firm to maintain accurate books and records. Each Partner shall have the right to inspect all financial records upon reasonable notice. Accounting fees shall be treated as a shared Partnership expense.

---

## ARTICLE 4 — CONTRIBUTED ASSETS

4.1 **Powell's Contributions.** Brad Powell / Elev8 AI contributes the following existing app concepts, designs, and intellectual property to the Partnership:
- All current app designs and concepts developed under the Elev8 AI Solutions & Services brand, including Lega-C-Lock and GemScout

4.2 **Spaulding's Contributions.** Francis Spaulding contributes the following existing assets to the Partnership:
- [APP NAME — to be completed by Spaulding] (fully developed)
- Development resources, tools, and team infrastructure

4.3 All contributed assets shall be jointly owned by the Partnership on a 50/50 basis upon execution of this Agreement.

---

## ARTICLE 5 — INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

5.1 All intellectual property, including but not limited to software, code, designs, trademarks, trade secrets, and concepts created during the term of this Partnership ("Partnership IP") shall be jointly owned 50/50 by the Partners.

5.2 Neither Partner may license, sell, transfer, or otherwise dispose of Partnership IP without the prior written consent of the other Partner.

5.3 Upon dissolution of the Partnership, ownership of Partnership IP shall be negotiated in good faith. If no agreement is reached, the matter shall proceed to arbitration per Article 9.

5.4 Each Partner retains sole ownership of any intellectual property created entirely outside the scope of the Partnership and not utilizing Partnership resources.

---

## ARTICLE 6 — DECISION MAKING

6.1 **Joint Decisions.** All major business decisions — including entering new contracts, incurring significant expenses, hiring, and releasing new products — require mutual written consent of both Partners.

6.2 **Day-to-Day Operations.** Each Partner may make routine operational decisions within their designated role without prior approval from the other Partner.

6.3 **Deadlock.** In the event of a deadlock on a material decision, the Partners shall attempt mediation before proceeding to arbitration per Article 9.

---

## ARTICLE 7 — CONFIDENTIALITY

7.1 Each Partner agrees to keep confidential all proprietary information, trade secrets, client lists, financial data, and business strategies of the Partnership and of the other Partner.

7.2 This confidentiality obligation shall survive termination of this Agreement for a period of **three (3) years**.

7.3 Neither Partner shall disclose confidential information to any third party without the prior written consent of the other Partner, except as required by law.

---

## ARTICLE 8 — NON-COMPETE / NON-SOLICITATION

8.1 During the term of this Agreement, neither Partner shall independently develop, market, or sell products or services that directly compete with Partnership Projects without the prior written consent of the other Partner.

8.2 For a period of **twelve (12) months** following termination of this Agreement, neither Partner shall solicit clients, customers, or employees of the Partnership for independent business purposes.

---

## ARTICLE 9 — DISPUTE RESOLUTION

9.1 The Partners agree to attempt to resolve any dispute through good-faith negotiation first.

9.2 If negotiation fails within 30 days, the Partners agree to non-binding mediation.

9.3 If mediation fails, disputes shall be resolved by binding arbitration in the State of Florida, under the rules of the American Arbitration Association (AAA).

9.4 The prevailing party in any arbitration or legal proceeding shall be entitled to recover reasonable attorney's fees and costs.

---

## ARTICLE 10 — TERM AND TERMINATION

10.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue until terminated by either Partner with **sixty (60) days' written notice** to the other Partner.

10.2 This Agreement may be terminated immediately upon:
- Material breach by either Partner that remains uncured for 30 days after written notice
- Mutual written agreement of both Partners
- Bankruptcy or insolvency of either Partner
- Death or permanent incapacity of either Partner (for individual Partners)

10.3 Upon termination, all outstanding revenue shall be distributed per the 50/50 split. Jointly owned IP disposition shall be handled per Article 5.3.

---

## ARTICLE 11 — REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

11.1 Each Partner represents and warrants that:
- They have full legal authority to enter into this Agreement
- This Agreement does not conflict with any existing employment, contract, or legal obligation
- Any assets contributed are free of liens, claims, or encumbrances

11.2 **Spaulding specifically warrants** that his participation in this Partnership does not violate any employment agreement or conflict-of-interest policy with AI4B or any other employer or client.

---

## ARTICLE 12 — GENERAL PROVISIONS

12.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Florida.

12.2 **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Partners and supersedes all prior discussions and agreements.

12.3 **Amendments.** This Agreement may only be amended by a written document signed by both Partners.

12.4 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

12.5 **Notices.** All notices shall be in writing and delivered by email with read receipt or certified mail to the addresses listed herein.

12.6 **No Waiver.** Failure to enforce any provision of this Agreement shall not constitute a waiver of that provision.
