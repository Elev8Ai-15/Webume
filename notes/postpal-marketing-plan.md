## 3. Strategic Roadmap

### Phase 1: Foundation and Launch (Months 1-3)
- Finalize pricing, implement subscriptions (RevenueCat)
- Optimize App Store/Google Play listings
- Launch on Product Hunt (Top 5 target)
- Begin organic social (daily TikTok/Reels)
- 12 SEO blog posts, 5 micro-influencer partnerships
- Goals: 8,000 downloads, 2,000 MAU, 100 paid

### Phase 2: Growth and Optimization (Months 4-9)
- A/B test pricing and onboarding
- Scale winning paid channels
- Launch Postpal Challenge UGC campaign
- 15 active influencer partnerships
- YouTube tutorial series, community building
- Goals: 25,000 downloads, 5,000 MAU, 500 paid

### Phase 3: Scale and Monetize (Months 10-18)
- Launch Business tier ($19.99/mo)
- Explore white-label for agencies
- International expansion (English-speaking)
- PR campaign, podcast tour (10+ appearances)
- Goals: 50,000 downloads, 8,000 MAU, 1,200 paid, $15K MRR
