Elev8 AI sells AI chatbots, automation workflows, and websites to small/medium businesses. The company is pre-revenue with small social followings and 17 assessment submissions. This plan uses a three-phase approach:

- **Phase 1 (Weeks 1-4):** Foundation -- build content engine, launch paid ads, begin direct outreach. Target: 2 paying clients, $2,500 MRR.
- **Phase 2 (Weeks 5-8):** Acceleration -- scale outreach, launch referral program, run local events. Target: 3 additional clients, $5,000 MRR.

The plan is designed for a one-person operator (Brad) spending 4-5 hours/day on marketing, supplemented by AI tools (HeyGen, scheduling tools, automation).
