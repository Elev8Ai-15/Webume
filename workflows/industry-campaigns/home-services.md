## TOP 5 PAIN POINTS

1. **Missed calls = missed jobs** — Average home service company misses 20-40% of calls
   - AI Fix: 24/7 chatbot handles service requests, gives quotes, books appointments
2. **Seasonal demand spikes** — Staff can't keep up during busy seasons (hurricane, summer AC)
   - AI Fix: AI scales instantly to handle unlimited simultaneous inquiries
3. **Quoting takes too long** — Customers want instant pricing, techs are in the field
   - AI Fix: AI provides instant ballpark estimates and schedules on-site quotes
4. **Scheduling chaos** — Double-bookings, missed windows, customer complaints
   - AI Fix: AI-powered scheduling that syncs with tech availability
5. **No reviews or weak online presence** — Customers trust Google reviews over everything
   - AI Fix: Automated post-service review request sequences
