# Professional Services Campaign Playbook
## Target: Accounting, Insurance, Financial Advisors, Consultants, Bookkeeping
## Tampa Bay Focus

## TOP 5 PAIN POINTS

1. **Client onboarding is slow and manual** — Document collection, forms, back-and-forth emails take weeks
   - AI Fix: Automated onboarding workflow — collects docs, sends reminders, tracks completion
2. **Repetitive client questions consume hours** — "When are taxes due?", "What do I need to bring?", "What's my premium?"
   - AI Fix: AI chatbot answers common questions instantly from knowledge base
3. **Seasonal overwhelm** — Tax season, open enrollment, year-end planning — staff maxed out
   - AI Fix: AI handles overflow inquiries, scheduling, and basic intake during peaks
4. **Lead follow-up is inconsistent** — Prospects who inquire don't hear back fast enough
   - AI Fix: Instant response + automated nurture sequence until consultation is booked
5. **Difficulty differentiating** — Every accountant/advisor/agent says the same thing online
   - AI Fix: Modern AI-powered website and chatbot positions them as innovative and tech-forward
