## TOP 5 PAIN POINTS

1. **Missed calls = missed patients** — 67% of after-hours callers never call back
   - AI Fix: 24/7 chatbot answers questions, books appointments, captures leads
2. **Front desk bottleneck** — Staff drowning in scheduling, insurance questions, follow-ups
   - AI Fix: Automate appointment booking, reminders, and FAQ responses
3. **No-shows bleeding revenue** — Average 20-30% no-show rate
   - AI Fix: Automated text/email reminders with easy reschedule links
4. **Slow follow-up on new patient inquiries** — Average response time: 47 hours
   - AI Fix: Instant automated response within seconds, 24/7
5. **Online reviews are weak** — Competitors have 100+ Google reviews, they have 15
   - AI Fix: Automated post-visit review request sequences
