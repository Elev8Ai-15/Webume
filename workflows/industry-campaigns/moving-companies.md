## TOP 5 PAIN POINTS

1. **Quote requests go unanswered for hours** — Someone requests a moving quote and doesn't hear back for half a day. They've already booked with the company that responded in 5 minutes.
   - AI Fix: Instant chatbot gives ballpark estimates, asks qualifying questions, books in-home estimates
2. **Phone staff can't handle volume during peak season** — May through September in Tampa Bay is moving season. Calls stack up, quotes don't go out, revenue walks.
   - AI Fix: AI scales instantly to handle unlimited simultaneous inquiries
3. **No online booking or instant quotes** — Most moving websites just have a phone number and a basic form. No way to get a ballpark estimate, no self-service scheduling.
   - AI Fix: AI chatbot collects move details and gives preliminary pricing range instantly
4. **Review management is critical and neglected** — Moving is emotional and high-stress. One bad experience generates a detailed negative review. Most companies don't actively manage reputation.
   - AI Fix: Automated post-move review request sequences
5. **Repeat/referral business left on the table** — Moving companies rarely follow up after the move. No review requests, no referral incentives.
   - AI Fix: Automated follow-up + referral program sequences
