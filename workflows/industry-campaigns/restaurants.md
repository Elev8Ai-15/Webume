## TOP 5 PAIN POINTS

1. **Phone tag for reservations** — Staff can't answer phones during rush, customers give up
   - AI Fix: 24/7 AI reservation assistant handles bookings, modifications, cancellations
2. **Repetitive customer questions** — Hours, menu, dietary options, parking, dress code — asked 50 times/day
   - AI Fix: AI chatbot answers all FAQs instantly from your website
3. **No-show epidemic** — Restaurants lose 15-20% of covers to no-shows
   - AI Fix: Automated confirmation/reminder sequences via text
4. **Event/catering inquiries fall through** — Large revenue opportunities lost to slow response
   - AI Fix: AI captures event details, provides initial pricing, schedules tastings
5. **Staffing crisis** — Can't hire enough hosts/front desk. Turnover is constant.
   - AI Fix: AI handles front-of-house communication so staff focuses on in-person guests
