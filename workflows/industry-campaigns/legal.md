## TOP 5 PAIN POINTS

1. **Intake bottleneck** — Potential clients call, get voicemail, hire the next firm that answers
   - AI Fix: 24/7 chatbot qualifies leads, captures case details, schedules consultations instantly
2. **Expensive missed leads** — Average cost per legal lead: $150-400. Missing one is expensive.
   - AI Fix: Instant response captures every lead, even at 2 AM
3. **Staff overwhelmed with unqualified calls** — Paralegals spend hours screening non-cases
   - AI Fix: AI pre-screens with qualifying questions before connecting to staff
4. **Client communication gaps** — Clients constantly calling for case status updates
   - AI Fix: Automated status updates, FAQ chatbot for common case questions
5. **Weak online presence** — Many firms have outdated websites that don't convert
   - AI Fix: Modern AI-powered website with chatbot, intake forms, and instant scheduling
