## TOP 5 PAIN POINTS

1. **Lead response time kills deals** — 50% of buyers go with the first agent who responds
   - AI Fix: Instant chatbot response qualifies buyers/sellers, schedules showings
2. **Drowning in admin work** — Agents spend 60% of time on non-selling tasks
   - AI Fix: Automate follow-ups, appointment scheduling, document collection
3. **Inconsistent follow-up** — Leads fall through the cracks during busy seasons
   - AI Fix: Automated nurture sequences that never forget a lead
4. **After-hours inquiries lost** — Buyers browse listings at night, agents sleep
   - AI Fix: 24/7 AI assistant answers property questions, captures buyer preferences
5. **Property management headaches** — Tenant requests, maintenance tickets, rent reminders
   - AI Fix: Automated tenant communication portal and maintenance ticketing
