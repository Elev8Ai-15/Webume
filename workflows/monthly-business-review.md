# Monthly Business Review
## Runs: Last Friday of each month
## Goal: Track progress toward $500k ARR target

---

## STEP 1 — Gather Data (10 min)
Brad collects:
- Monthly revenue (invoices, Stripe dashboard)
- New clients signed
- Clients lost / churned
- Total active clients
- Assessment submissions this month
- Website traffic (if available)
- Social media follower counts across all platforms

---

## STEP 2 — Generate Report (10 min)
**Agent**: Financial Analyst + Analytics Reporter

**Prompt**:
```
Act as my Financial Analyst for Elev8 AI Solutions & Services.
Generate the monthly business review for [MONTH YEAR].

TARGET: $500k ARR by December 2026
That means: ~$41,667/month revenue needed

This month's data:
- Revenue: $[AMOUNT]
- New clients: [NUMBER]
- Lost clients: [NUMBER]
- Total active clients: [NUMBER]
- Average revenue per client: $[AMOUNT]
- Assessment submissions: [NUMBER]
- Conversion rate (assessments to clients): [X]%

Social media growth:
- LinkedIn followers: [NUMBER] (last month: [NUMBER])
- Facebook page likes: [NUMBER] (last month: [NUMBER])
- Instagram followers: [NUMBER] (last month: [NUMBER])
- TikTok followers: [NUMBER] (last month: [NUMBER])

Provide:
1. Revenue vs target (on track / behind / ahead)
2. Months remaining and required monthly growth rate
3. Client acquisition rate needed
4. Top revenue source this month
5. Biggest risk to hitting target
6. 3 specific actions for next month
7. Social media ROI assessment (which platform drives most leads?)
```

**Output**: Save to `outputs/reports/[MONTH]-[YEAR]-business-review.md`
