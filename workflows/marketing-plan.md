## COLD EMAIL INFRASTRUCTURE

**Setup (do this Week 1):**
1. Buy a secondary sending domain (e.g., go-elev8ai.com) to protect main domain reputation
2. Warm the domain for 2-3 weeks before sending volume
3. Use an email sending tool: Instantly.ai, Smartlead, or Lemlist
4. Send 25-50 emails/day per sending account
5. Rotate 2-3 sending accounts
6. Verify all emails before sending (NeverBounce or ZeroBounce)

**Negative keywords for lists:** free chatbot, chatbot tutorial, build your own, DIY, jobs, career, salary

## GOOGLE MAPS PROSPECTING (Weekly, 1 hour)

1. Open Google Maps
2. Search: "[industry] near Tampa Bay" (e.g., "HVAC near Tampa Bay")
3. Review each business for:
   - No website (immediate high-value prospect)
   - Bad/outdated website (no mobile, no chat, no booking)
   - No reviews or poor reviews
   - No Google Business profile optimization
4. Record: business name, owner name, phone, email, website, notes
5. Add to outreach list for cold email and LinkedIn campaigns
6. **Weekly target: 25-50 new prospects**
