I spent 25 years in retail operations. Running stores, managing teams, building systems that kept things moving. It was good work and I was good at it.

But about a year ago I started seeing something I couldn't ignore.

Every small business owner I talked to — doctors, lawyers, contractors, real estate agents — was dealing with the same problems I'd been solving in retail for decades. Missed leads, clunky processes, too much manual work, not enough hours in the day.

The difference was they didn't have a 200-person operations team to fix it. They had themselves. Maybe one or two employees. And a website that was basically a glorified business card.

So I started building for them.

Not consulting. Not advising. Actually sitting down and building the chatbots, the automation workflows, the websites that do real work. And delivering the finished product in days, not months.

That's Elev8 AI Solutions. Three months in, I'm more energized by this work than I've been in years. Turns out the skills you build running retail operations at scale translate pretty well when you're building AI-powered systems for small businesses.

If you're thinking about making a similar leap — from a career you've outgrown into something that actually excites you — I'm happy to talk about what that transition looks like. It's not as scary as it sounds.

And if you're a Tampa Bay business owner who's been meaning to figure out the AI thing, my door's open. elev8ai.org
