Q1 is almost over.

Before you start planning Q2 spend, ask yourself these 3 questions:

1. How many calls did we miss last quarter because no one was available to pick up?

2. How many hours did our front desk spend on tasks that could be handled automatically — scheduling, follow-ups, answering the same 10 questions?

3. How many leads went cold because we took too long to respond?

If the answer to any of these is "too many" — that's not a people problem. That's a systems problem.

I work with Tampa Bay service businesses — healthcare practices, law firms, home services companies — and I see the same pattern every time:

Great people. Good intentions. Zero automation.

The businesses that win in Q2 aren't the ones that hire more staff. They're the ones that give their existing team better tools.

An AI chatbot that answers questions at 2 AM. Automated follow-ups that go out in seconds, not days. A booking system that never sleeps.

That's the kind of Q2 upgrade that actually moves the needle.
